## 1. Executive Summary & Core Rules

1. **Duration & Scope**:
   - Applies to new / mid-year student admissions for the remaining **7 academic months** (September to March).
2. **Pricing Formula**:
   $$\text{7-Month Total Fee} = (\text{Monthly Tuition Rate} \times 7) + ₹1,000 \text{ (Registration Fee)}$$
3. **Instalment Model**:
   - **Option 1: One Time Payment (OTP)**: Upfront payment with ~10% to 11% discount.
   - **Option 2: 5 Monthly Instalments**: Structured payments (`1st`, `2nd`, `3rd`, `4th`, `5th`) due on monthly rolling offsets (+0, +1, +2, +3, +4 months from enrollment date).
   - **Exact Parity**: $\sum (\text{1st} + \text{2nd} + \text{3rd} + \text{4th} + \text{5th}) = \text{7-Month Total Fee}$ (100% mathematical match).
